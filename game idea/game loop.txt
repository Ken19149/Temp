Lore: 
Once upon a time, there was a wise sage who ruled the world, united all nations, and brought peace upon the lands.
"Knowledge is power"—the most famous quote ever said by a once renowned legend.

One day, the grand sage suddenly disappeared—forever— leaving only one last message: 
"There shall come a day when all of humanity live in harmony. Until that day, I may leave the land to you, the new ruler—grand sage"

Since the news spread, nations break apart, all who interpreted the message try to become the new ruler.
As the sage has said, until that day, I may leave the land to you...
