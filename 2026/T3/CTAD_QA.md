# discovery: 

dessert = peppermint  
toothpaste = spearmint  
  
peppermint = strong (high menthol)  
spearmint = mild (low menthol)  
  
---

supertasters (25% of population) = ppl with many taste buds (35 or more in 6mm circle) // sensitive to taste  
medium tasters (50% of population) = 15-35 taste buds  
non-tasters (25% of population) =  < 15 taste buds // like intense flavors like spicy food  
  
---
  
Taste = sweet, sour, salty, bitter, umami
Chemesthesis = chemical sensation (pain, touch, thermal reception, etc.)
- chili peppers = burn (capsaicin)  
- peppermint = cooling (menthol)  
- carbonated soda = tingle / fizzy (carbonated acid)  
- black pepper = heat (piperine)  
- wasabi = tear-inducing sting (allyl isothiocyanate)
